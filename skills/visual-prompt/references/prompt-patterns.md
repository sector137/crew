# Prompt Patterns — Software Sal Visual Universe

---

## The Style Prefix

**Copy this verbatim at the start of every prompt:**

```
2D adult animated sci-fi sitcom style, flat vibrant colors, crisp thick black outlines, slightly cynical and exaggerated character design, clean vector-like animation aesthetic. The lighting is flat but cinematic.
```

This prefix is the DNA of every visual asset. It anchors all outputs to the same aesthetic universe. Never modify it. Never replace any part of it.

---

## What the Style Prefix Produces

- **Flat color fills** — no gradients, no photorealistic shading
- **Thick black outlines** — all forms are clearly separated
- **Exaggerated proportions** — character design reads as stylized, not realistic
- **Clean vector-like quality** — crisp edges, intentional shapes
- **Cinematic flat lighting** — mood through composition and color, not light simulation
- **Adult animated register** — not children's cartoon, not hyperrealism. Think: *Archer*, *Futurama*, *Rick and Morty* at their most serious

## What to Avoid

**Words that degrade the aesthetic:**
- realistic, photorealistic, hyperdetailed, cinematic realism
- oil painting, watercolor, sketch (unless specifically for a study version)
- 3D render, CGI, Pixar style
- anime (too different in aesthetic register)
- cute, kawaii, chibi
- dark fantasy, gritty, grungy

**Colors to avoid outside the HUD palette:**
- Random pastels not in the system
- Earth tones not in the system (browns other than Copper)
- Bright saturated primaries that clash (pure red, primary blue, bright yellow-green)

**Composition approaches to avoid:**
- Cluttered backgrounds with too many elements
- Symmetrical compositions (feels static, not dynamic)
- Centered subjects without negative space
- Multiple conflicting light sources

## Enhancement Techniques

**For characters — push personality:**
- Specify the exact emotion in their expression
- Name the physical tell of their personality ("the stillness of Kael", "Harlan leaning forward")
- Include a signature prop or environmental element

**For environments — establish scale:**
- Include a scale reference (a panel that looks human-sized, a door, a seat)
- Specify whether the space is occupied or empty
- Describe what's visible through windows or in the distance

**For marketing art — establish narrative:**
- Describe what's just happened or is about to happen
- Specify the emotional register (tense, triumphant, focused, sardonic)
- Note the composition framing (hero shot, ensemble, dramatic reveal)
